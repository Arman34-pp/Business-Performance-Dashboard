Excited to share my latest Power BI project — an end-to-end Business    Performance Dashboard built to track sales, orders, customers, and product performance across regions in real time. 

📊 Key Highlights:
 ✅ Multi-page interactive dashboard (6 pages) covering KPI summaries, comparative analysis, and drill-through tooltips
 ✅ Real-time KPI tracking: Total Sales, Orders, Quantity Sold & Customers with YoY growth indicators
 ✅ Target vs Actual performance gauges for Sales, Orders, Quantity, and Customers
 ✅ Geo-spatial analysis with Total Sales by City (Bing Maps integration)
 ✅ Product category breakdown via dynamic bar & donut charts
 ✅ Top 100 Customers ranking table with conditional formatting
 ✅ State-level filtering (dynamic slicers) for regional drill-downs
 ✅ Custom tooltip pages for enhanced UX on hover

📈 Business Impact / Key Metrics:
 • 💰 Total Sales: 688.56K (+40.61%)
• 📦 Total Quantity Sold: 7,019 (+30.95%)
• 🛒 Total Orders: 1,255 (+32.80%)
• 👥 Total Customers: 582 (+25.45%)
